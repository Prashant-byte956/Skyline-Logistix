What We Move

Freight solutions for every kind of load

From single pallets to full truckloads and oversized industrial equipment — one network handles it all.

FTL

Full Truckload

Dedicated vehicles for bulk consignments, moving direct from origin to destination without transshipment.

LTL

Part Load

Cost-efficient shared-load freight for smaller consignments on regular trunk routes across India.

XPS

Express Delivery

Time-committed dispatches for urgent, high-priority shipments that can't wait for a regular schedule.

ODC

Specialized vehicles, route surveys and permits for heavy, oversized or industrial equipment.

WHS

Short and long-term storage with inventory handling and dispatch support at strategic hubs.

RVL

Return, replacement and pick-up management built for e-commerce and retail operations.
